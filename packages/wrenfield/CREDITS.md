# Credits

All product imagery and 3D models come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) (public domain). No attribution is required; we give it anyway, with thanks to the artists below.

The product photos are Poly Haven's own preview renders of these CC0 models (the main render and the orthographic front, side and top renders), saved as WebP. The files in `models/` are the 1k glTF versions, packed as GLB with [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev), textures converted to WebP, and in some cases scaled to the catalogue dimensions.

Wrenfield is fictional. Product names, makers, origins, prices and descriptions are invented and do not describe the original assets.
