One of a kind

Holding an antique

Every antique is the only one. When you add one to your bag we hold it for you for 15 minutes, and your bag shows until when. If you have not ordered by then, the piece leaves your bag and goes back on sale. Once you order, it is marked sold.

Returns

If something is not right

Changing your mind

You can return an antique within 14 days of delivery, as long as it comes back as it arrived. Tell us within the 14 days and we arrange the collection, charged at the delivery fee for your zone. The price of the piece is refunded in full once it is back in the workshop.

Made-to-order pieces

These are made for you, in the finish you chose, so they cannot be returned for a change of mind. You can cancel within 7 days of ordering and your deposit is refunded in full. After that, the workshop has begun.

Damage in transit

Please check each piece when it arrives; the white-glove team will wait while you do. Tell us about any damage within 48 hours, with a photograph, and we will repair or replace the piece at no cost to you.

Refunds

Refunds go back the way you paid, by bank transfer or as a credit against your invoice, within 5 working days of the piece reaching us.

Care

Looking after your pieces

Wood

Keep wood out of strong sun and away from radiators and fires, and dust it with a soft, dry cloth. Our own pieces are finished in hard wax oil and our antiques in wax; refresh either once or twice a year. Use mats and coasters, as heat and water mark any finish.

Leather

Aniline and semi-aniline leathers darken and soften with use. Keep them out of direct sun, blot spills at once with a dry cloth, and feed the leather with a neutral conditioner twice a year.

Marble and stone

Marble and limestone are porous. Wipe up wine, citrus and coffee straight away, as they etch the surface, and clean with warm water and a drop of pH-neutral soap. Reseal the stone once a year.

Brass and bronze

Our brass is not lacquered, so it darkens to a soft patina. Leave it, or brighten it with brass polish and a soft cloth. Bronze needs only dusting and, once a year, a thin coat of clear wax.

Linen and velvet

Brush or vacuum gently with a soft attachment, and turn loose cushions every week or two so they wear evenly. Blot spills rather than rub them.

Lighting

Every light is rewired and tested before it leaves us. Use the bulb type and wattage on its label, and have a qualified electrician hang chandeliers and wall lights.

Antiques

An antique has already lived through more than a century of seasons. Keep it at an even temperature and humidity, and lift it rather than drag it. A hairline in old timber, or a drawer that runs tight in damp weather, is part of its age, not a fault.
