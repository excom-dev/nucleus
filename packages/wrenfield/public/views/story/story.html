Made to order

The workshop

Our workshop is a former coach works on the edge of the Somerset Levels. Six cabinetmakers make the tables, chests and shelving that carry the Wrenfield Workshop name, in solid oak, walnut and cherry, jointed and finished by hand.

The rest of our new pieces come from six workshops we have known for years: upholsterers in Portugal and England, cabinetmakers in Denmark, a stone workshop in Belgium, a foundry in Italy and a pottery in the West Country. Each piece carries the name of the workshop that made it.

Nothing is made until you order it. You choose the finish, pay half as a deposit, and the piece is made for you in four to twelve weeks. The lead time for each finish is shown beside it.

The antiques room

Our buyers look for furniture, lighting and objects made between about 1840 and 1920: French seating, English oak and mahogany, Chinese elm, brass from France and the Netherlands. We buy only what we would keep ourselves.

Every piece comes back to the workshop before it goes on sale, and we do only what it needs. Old surfaces are cleaned and waxed rather than stripped. Original handles, knobs and frames stay. Seats are re-upholstered by hand, the traditional way, and every light is rewired to current standards. Patina is left as we find it.

Each antique is one of a kind. When you put one in your bag we hold it for you for 15 minutes, and once it is ordered it is marked sold.
